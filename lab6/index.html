RISE AND GRIND

The Legend of Meemaw's Starter

Every single loaf of bread pulled from the ovens here at Rise and Grind shares a single heartbeat. Our baking lineage traces all the way back to my great-great-great-great-great Meemaw, who cultivated an extraordinary wild yeast culture generations ago.

Through cross-country travels, challenging winters, and the passing of decades, her ancient sourdough starter was carefully kept alive, fed daily like a cherished family member. Today, we carry on that exact tradition. When you taste our bread, you aren't just eating standard ingredients—you are experiencing over a century of history, dedication, and Meemaw's timeless legacy.
